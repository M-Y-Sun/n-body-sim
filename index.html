N-Body Simulation





Click the display to add a body.

Click the body to show options to edit the body. When vectors are shown, click and drag the space around the selected body to graphically set its velocity.

Bodies can be moved or deleted with their respective buttons on the selection popup. Deletion can also be done with the backspace key.

Decreasing the θ value increases the accuracy of the simulation. Simulation may become jerky with a high θ value.

Note: Enabling vectors and/or paths while running may cause additional CPU pressure. Your computer may consumer more power while these graphics are enabled.




Drag the selected body to change its position.


Click "Finish" when you are done.
